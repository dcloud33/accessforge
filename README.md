# accessforge
Employee access lifecycle platform demonstrating policy-based authorization access reconciliation, reliable provisioning workflows, and audit evidence

## Business Problem
Employees may retain access to internal applications after changing teams or leaving the company. When access updates fail or are missed, outdated permissions can expose sensitive information to unauthorized access. AccessForge is being developed to compare employees’ actual access against the access required by their current employment status and department. It will track incomplete changes and verify that required permissions are granted and outdated permissions are removed.
